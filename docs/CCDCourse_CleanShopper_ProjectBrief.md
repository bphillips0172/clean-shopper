# Clean Shopper
## Demo Project Brief — Claude Code for Designers

---

## What It Is

Clean Shopper is an AI-powered product that helps people discover home and pantry products that are clean, non-toxic, and environmentally friendly. Users can research products, compare options based on ingredient safety and sustainability, save their preferences, and build a shopping list or cart.

The format is up to you. Clean Shopper can be a web app, a mobile app, a conversational assistant, or a combination. Choose the form that best fits the experience you want to design, and record your choice in your design notes.

It is the course demo project for Claude Code for Designers. Students build it over the course using Claude Code, with the instructor demonstrating the full workflow live in each session.

---

## Target User

Someone who wants to make more informed purchasing decisions for their home and family -- replacing toxic cleaning products, finding personal care items without harmful chemicals, or stocking a pantry with cleaner food options -- but finds the research overwhelming and time-consuming.

Key frustrations the product addresses:

- Product labels are confusing and ingredient lists require expertise to interpret
- "Clean" and "natural" claims on packaging are often misleading or unverified
- Researching alternatives across multiple sources is fragmented and slow
- Preferences built up over time -- trusted brands, avoided ingredients -- have nowhere to live

---

## Feature Scope (V1)

**Product research**
Users describe or search for what they're looking for, and Clean Shopper researches options, evaluates ingredients against clean standards, and surfaces recommendations with reasoning. It can draw on sources like EWG's Skin Deep for ingredient safety data.

**Preference management**
Users save their preferences: ingredients to avoid, trusted brands, certifications that matter to them (EWG Verified, USDA Organic, B Corp, etc.). Clean Shopper applies these to every subsequent recommendation automatically.

**Shopping list or cart**
Recommended products can be saved to a list or cart that persists across sessions.

**Comparison**
Users can compare products side by side and get a clear recommendation based on their saved preferences.

Out of scope for V1: checkout or payment processing, direct retailer integrations, barcode scanning, user accounts or authentication.
