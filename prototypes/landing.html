How it works

Research in minutes, not hours

Reading ingredient labels across a dozen products takes an afternoon. Clean Shopper handles the research so you can make a confident choice in minutes.

1

Describe what you need

Tell Clean Shopper what you're looking for — a laundry detergent free of fragrances, a face moisturizer for sensitive skin, a pasta sauce without seed oils. No need to know the ingredient names.

2

Get scored recommendations

Clean Shopper researches options, evaluates every ingredient against clean standards, and returns a shortlist with a plain-language explanation of each one's strengths and concerns.

3

Save and build your list

Add the products you like to your shopping list. Save your preferences — ingredients to avoid, trusted brands, certifications that matter — and Clean Shopper applies them to every future search.

Features

Everything you need to shop clean

Four tools designed around the real frustrations of ingredient research.

Product research

Describe what you're looking for in plain language. Clean Shopper evaluates ingredients against EWG Skin Deep, flags concerns, and surfaces options with clear reasoning — not just ratings.

Side-by-side comparison

Compare products directly on any dimension that matters to you. Ingredient safety, certification status, fragrance-free status — filtered through your saved preferences, not generic criteria.

Preference management

Save the ingredients you avoid, the brands you trust, and the certifications that matter to you. Clean Shopper applies these automatically so you never have to repeat yourself.

Shopping list

Recommended products save to a list that persists across sessions. Build out your home, personal care, and pantry categories over time as you find products you trust.
